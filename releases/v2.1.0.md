# GeoDex 2.1.0

Ranks now run much longer. There is a new rank every 50,000 XP, eleven in all, from Tourist to Atlas keeper at 500,000. Wayfarer, Pathfinder, Surveyor and World scholar join the old ones. The old top rank came at 25,300 XP, about two weeks of daily study.

A day now holds at most 75 reviews, and new cards fill what is left up to 100. A day with many reviews brings fewer new cards, so reviews no longer pile up. The limit can be 50, 75, 100, 150 or none in Settings, above the new-card limit. When the limit holds reviews back, Today says how many are waiting, and Keep going offers ten more reviews above ten new cards.
