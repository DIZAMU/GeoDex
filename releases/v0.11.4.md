# Phase 11.4: settings for your goals

Settings now follow what you want: your goals first, then what you want to learn.

Features
- Settings are grouped into Your goals, What you want to learn, Study and Guidance
- Daily goal: reach your daily time, or finish 1, 2 or 3 sessions a day. The goal ring on Today follows it
- Meeting your goal earns XP and counts the day for your streak, even with reviews left
- Choose your default session length and focus; Today's picker starts there
- Replay the guided tour, or see the welcome page again, from Settings

Tests: unit tests for session goals (finished sessions, days, goal XP) and for streak days kept by the goal; an end-to-end test that changes each goal setting and checks Today.
