# Phase 11.1: welcome, guidance and help

From a real user test: a new visitor now learns what GeoDex is, that it is free, what they can learn and how to start, and is shown around.

Features
- Welcome page for first-time visitors: what GeoDex is, "Free. No ads, no account, no tracking", what you can learn (with counts), what you get, how long it takes at your daily time, how it works in three steps, the level path from 1 to 10, and a spinning globe that lights up as a map would
- Try 3 questions on the welcome page (a map tap, a flag and a capital) with real feedback and a memory hook; nothing is saved
- The placement test is offered, not forced: take the 5-minute test, or start from zero
- A guided tour after setup points out where to start, how to answer, your map, the Atlas, Stats, Settings and Help; skip it anytime, replay it from Help or Settings
- "What is GeoDex?" on Today: open for your first week, then one line that opens on tap
- A help button on every screen opens Help: a clickable map of the app, how answers are graded, why questions get harder, why cards come back, the keyboard, and the level path
- Settings can show the welcome page and tour again

Tests: an end-to-end first visit in English and Norwegian on desktop and phone (free and topics visible without scrolling, the demo, start from zero, every tour step, Help from every screen), unit tests for the time estimate, and screenshots of the welcome page and Help in every theme and language.
