# GeoDex 2.0.9

The turning globe behind questions you answer from a list now stays when you answer. It turns the shorter way to the place and zooms in, with the answer filled in and a wrong pick marked in red, instead of jumping to the flat map.

Also in this release
- A card you missed once no longer keeps coming back after right answers. A right answer that was slow, or a city pin a little off, used to keep it in its short repeat step, so the same cards returned every few minutes. Now a right answer always moves it on.
