# Phase 6: famous cities

A short list of world-famous cities, and the ones people mistake for capitals.

Cities
- 38 cities: New York, Shanghai, Mumbai, Barcelona, Venice, Kyoto and more, plus every city commonly mistaken for its country's capital
- Two cards per city: which country it is in, and where it is (tap the map, graded by distance: within 100 km is right, within 300 km is close)
- A city starts only after its country, and cities share the daily new cards with countries
- Off by default: turn on Famous cities in Settings

Not the capital
- Sydney, Toronto, Istanbul, Zurich, Rio and the rest each get a card asking for the real capital, after you have studied the city
- The map shows both: the capital and the city mistaken for it

Atlas and stats
- Country pages list their famous cities with your level
- Stats shows a Famous cities row per continent when the module is on

Content
- Hooks and facts for every city in English and Norwegian, written per language

Tests: 550 unit, 44 end-to-end, including every not-the-capital pair producing a working card.
