# Phase 5.2: Atlas globe

The Atlas map is now a globe you can spin in any direction.

Atlas
- Drag to turn the globe; a flick keeps it spinning and slows down on its own
- Scroll or pinch to zoom, arrow keys to turn, hover to see a country's name
- Opens facing your home country
- Coastlines draw lighter while the globe moves and in full detail when it stops
- A Globe and Flat map toggle keeps the flat view one tap away

Fixes
- Cloudflare preview builds of pull requests deploy again

Tests: 532 unit, plus end-to-end checks for tapping, dragging, inertia, zoom and the view toggle.
