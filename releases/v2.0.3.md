# GeoDex 2.0.3

The end of a session is clearer. The last question's button says Finish, a quick placeholder of your results shows while they are counted, and the results open with a row of dots, one for each answer, so you can see at a glance how it went.

Levels move sooner. Your level is how much you remember of everything in your topics, and it used to need hundreds of cards before level 2. The first levels now come within a few sessions, while level 10 still means every card mastered. The level path marks the new milestones: every card started is level 4, every card holding a week level 7.

Only one thing is called a level now. XP no longer shows a level number of its own: it shows your rank and the XP to the next one, such as "1,776 XP to Navigator".

Also in this release
- The progress bar is full on the last question, instead of one step short of "5 of 5"
- The least known facts are labelled "Bonus fact" on a line of their own, instead of "Deeper cut" squeezed in front of the text
