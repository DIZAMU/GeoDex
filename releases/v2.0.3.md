# GeoDex 2.0.3

The end of a session is clearer. The last question's button says Finish, a quick placeholder of your results shows while they are counted, and the results open with a row of dots, one for each answer, so you can see at a glance how it went.

Also in this release
- The progress bar is full on the last question, instead of one step short of "5 of 5"
- The least known facts are labelled "Bonus fact" on a line of their own, instead of "Deeper cut" squeezed in front of the text
