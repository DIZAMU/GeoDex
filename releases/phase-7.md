# Phase 7: landmarks

120 landmarks from every inhabited continent, each with a photo from Wikimedia Commons.

Landmarks
- 120 landmarks: the Eiffel Tower, Machu Picchu, the Taj Mahal, Uluru, Petra, Preikestolen and more, at least 14 per continent
- Three cards per landmark: name it from its photo, say which country it is in, and place it on the map (within 150 km is right, within 500 km is close)
- Each landmark is in exactly one country: places on a border (Victoria Falls, Niagara, Everest) are left out
- A landmark starts after its country, and landmarks share the daily new cards with the other modules
- Off by default: turn on Landmarks in Settings

Photos
- One Commons photo per landmark, public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA only
- Every photo shows its author and license, linked to its Commons page
- Photos load from Wikimedia the first time and work offline after that; they are not part of the app download

Atlas and stats
- Country pages list their landmarks with your level
- Stats shows a Landmarks row per continent when the module is on

Content
- Hooks and facts for every landmark in English and Norwegian, written per language, listed in REVIEW.md for checking

Tests: unit tests for the data, grading and every landmark card; end-to-end for all three cards in both languages, photo credits and offline photos.
