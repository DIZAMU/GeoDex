# GeoDex 2.0.2

The globe is back on Today. It turns slowly and shows how far you have come: each country takes the colour of its level, and the region you are about to study is marked. Tap a country to open its page.

Also in this release
- The focus list on Today and in Settings opens below its button and scrolls, instead of covering the page
- Settings say "Changes saved" each time a change is stored
- Over time in the Atlas shows each year's borders on the globe, and the globe turns to them as you change year
- The flat map no longer slides sideways at full size; you can zoom in, and move only within the map
- A map that zooms to a country at the edge of the world no longer jumps when you first drag it
- On narrow phones the level path no longer overlaps itself, and the cards on a country's page keep their names on one line
