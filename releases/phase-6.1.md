# Phase 6.1: groups of countries

Questions and map placement for named groups: Scandinavia, the Caribbean, the Balkans, the EU and 35 more.

Groups
- 39 groups: 21 named regions and organizations (Scandinavia, Nordic countries, Baltic states, Benelux, Iberian Peninsula, Balkans, South Caucasus, Middle East, Levant, Maghreb, Horn of Africa, Arabian Peninsula, Arab Gulf states, Indian subcontinent, Indochina, Korean Peninsula, Central America, Visegrád Group, G7, EU, NATO) and 18 UN subregions (Caribbean, Western Africa, South-eastern Asia, Polynesia and the rest)
- Three cards per group: tap every member on the map, name the highlighted group, and pick the member among countries outside it
- Tapping is graded by slips: all members and nothing else is right, one miss or wrong tap is close, more is wrong
- Disputed members (Greece in the Balkans, Finland in Scandinavia) are shown on the answer but never asked and never count against you
- A group starts once half its countries are started, and groups share the daily new cards with countries and cities
- Off by default: turn on Groups of countries in Settings

Content
- A note for every named group in English and Norwegian, listed in REVIEW.md for checking

Tests: unit tests for grading, distractors, the plan and every group question; end-to-end for all three cards in both languages.
