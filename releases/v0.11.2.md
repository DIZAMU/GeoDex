# Phase 11.2: session picker

Sessions now have a size and a subject. Pick 5, 10 or 20 questions and what to practise, and the bar tells you where you are.

Features
- Today has a session picker: length 5, 10 or 20 questions, and a focus. Mixed takes everything due today; or pick one skill (location, capitals, flags, currency and language), one topic you have turned on (countries, cities, groups, landmarks, physical geography, history), a continent or one of its regions
- Each focus shows how many cards it holds today; empty ones are greyed out
- The start button says what you get: "Start 10 questions"
- Due reviews come first, then new cards, both within the focus
- A focused session tells you how many reviews wait outside it, so nothing slips quietly
- The session holds exactly the number you chose, and the bar reads "3 of 10"
- When a session ends with cards still waiting today, "Another 10 questions" starts the next round in the same focus
- Your last length and focus are remembered on this device

Tests: unit tests for the session slice (length, every focus kind, due before new, no entity back to back) and the session limit; end-to-end tests for a 5-question session, a focused session, another round, and the picker on desktop and phone in every theme and language.
