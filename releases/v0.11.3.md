# Phase 11.3: levels, XP and the overview

Progress you can see at a glance: a memory level from 1 to 10, XP and ranks that only go up, and a small celebration when you climb.

Features
- Memory level 1 to 10 for everything you study, each topic, each continent and each country. It shows how much you hold right now, so it drops when you forget, and it tells you how far the next level is ("6 more cards to level 2")
- Your cards count by how long they hold: started, a week, three weeks, two months
- XP for correct answers (more for typing and map answers than for choices), finished sessions (more for longer ones), each study day, meeting your daily time, and each time a card holds for a week, three weeks and two months
- XP levels with ranks: Tourist, Traveller, Explorer, Navigator, Cartographer, Geographer and Atlas keeper
- Today has an overview: your level ring with the next milestone, the path from 1 to 10, your rank and XP, today's goal ring, and a bar per topic
- Stats shows your level per continent and topic; each country page shows that country's level
- The session summary shows the XP you earned, and celebrates a new memory level or rank. It respects reduced motion
- The guided tour points out the overview

Tests: unit tests for memory levels (the ramp, level-downs, the cards to the next level), XP (each source, order independence, only going up) and the course's cards; end-to-end tests for the overview, country and continent levels, and a rank-up celebration, with screenshots in every theme and language on desktop and phone.
