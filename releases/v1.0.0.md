# GeoDex 1.0

GeoDex is complete: a free spaced-repetition geography trainer that learns what you confuse and explains why, in English and Norwegian, offline-first.

What 1.0 holds
- 197 countries with location, name, capital, flag, currency and official languages, plus famous cities, groups of countries, landmarks with photos, physical geography and a history layer with borders over time
- FSRS scheduling graded automatically from your answers and your speed, with questions that get harder as cards get stronger
- Explanations chosen for each answer: memory hooks, facts and comparisons with your home country, and contrast cards for the pairs you mix up
- A welcome page with a demo, an optional placement test, a guided tour and Help
- Sessions of 5, 10 or 20 questions on everything due or on one skill, topic or region
- Memory levels from 1 to 10, XP and ranks, a daily goal in minutes or sessions, streaks with freezes and achievements
- Atlas, country pages, stats and drills that never touch your schedule
- Works offline, syncs between devices through your own free Supabase project if you want, and exports everything as JSON
- Three themes and full keyboard and screen reader support

New in this release
- The way to the next memory level shows cards to go or a percentage. Tap it on Today to switch, or choose in Settings; the choice is remembered
- On a phone, the overview places your level text below the rings so long words never break
- The repository's contributor guide is now `CONTRIBUTING.md`
