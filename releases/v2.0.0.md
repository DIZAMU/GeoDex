# GeoDex 2.0

GeoDex 2.0 is simpler to start and calmer to look at. There are no accounts, the first screen is a real question, and every screen has been redrawn in one design for light and dark.

What changed
- No accounts and no sync. Progress stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere. Settings has a backup file to download and restore, and shows when you last made one; backups from 1.0 restore as before
- A first visit that starts with a map question you can answer right away, then your language and home country, and you are in. The placement test is gone
- A five step tour that ends by starting your first session. You pick the session length in the tour itself
- One design in light, dark or matching your device: one type family, ink blue only where it matters, no glows or boxed cards, and text that meets WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both modes
- On phones, a bottom bar with Today, Atlas, Stats, Settings and Help
- Today puts the cards due and the start button first, then the map, your level and one line for XP. The full level and XP picture is in Stats
- Stats reads top to bottom: your level and XP, what is coming up, what you remember, what you mix up and the cards that keep slipping
- Settings is a list of rows, each saying what it changes, with switches for topics
- The session summary lists the cards you will see again soon and what you mixed them up with
- A short tone and a buzz after each answer, both on by default and both in Settings
- Country pages no longer zoom out to the whole world when a neighbour is across an ocean (France and Brazil)
- Botswana and Zambia are now neighbours: their border at Kazungula is about 150 m long, too short for the map's geometry
- Every hook, fact and contrast is checked against the country data on each change: capitals, borders, coasts, official languages, currencies, and largest or smallest claims

Coming from 1.0
- Your progress on this device stays. Atlas becomes Dark; Minimal and Playful become Light
- Sync is gone. To move progress to another device, download a backup in Settings and restore it there
