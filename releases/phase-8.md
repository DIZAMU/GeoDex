# Phase 8: physical geography

91 oceans, seas, rivers, mountain ranges, deserts and lakes, drawn on the map from Natural Earth.

Features
- 5 oceans, 20 seas (gulfs and bays included), 25 rivers, 17 mountain ranges, 12 deserts and 12 lakes: the Nile, the Amazon, the Alps, the Himalayas, the Sahara, Lake Baikal, the North Sea and more
- Two cards per feature: tap it on the map, and name the highlighted one
- A new card frames the feature with room around it; later reviews show its continent, then the whole world. Oceans always show the world
- Rivers have a wide tap area, so a thin line is easy to hit; taps prefer rivers, then lakes, then land features, seas and oceans
- Water and feature names are set in Newsreader Italic
- A feature starts once a country it crosses or borders is started (oceans with your first country), and physical cards share the daily new cards with the other modules
- Off by default: turn on Physical geography in Settings

Atlas and stats
- Country pages list the features that cross or border them, with your level
- Stats shows a Physical geography row per continent when the module is on

Content
- Hooks and facts for every feature in English and Norwegian, written per language, listed in REVIEW.md for checking

Tests: unit tests for the data, the plan and every physical card; end-to-end proof that every feature renders and can be tapped at its default zoom, on desktop and phone, plus screenshots in every theme and language.
